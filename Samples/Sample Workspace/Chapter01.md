# Chapter One

The lamp had been burning since four, and by six the wick needed trimming.
She left it alone. There was a particular quality to a failing light that
suited the work, and she had learned not to interrupt it.

Outside, the road was still wet from the afternoon. A cart went past and
did not stop.

> Nothing arrives on the day you expect it.

She wrote that down, looked at it, and crossed it out.
