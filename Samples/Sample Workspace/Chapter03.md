# Chapter Three

The surveyor arrived on the Tuesday with a case of instruments and an
opinion about the weather (which he offered before he had taken off his
coat.

She let him talk. People who are certain about the weather are usually
certain about other things too, and it is quicker to let them finish.

"You will want the north field drained," he said.

She had had the north field drained twice. She did not say so.

By evening he had walked the boundary and come back with with a figure
that was almost exactly the figure her father had given her, twenty
years earlier, standing in the same doorway.
